    About

    Attendance Planner is a student productivity tool designed to help you stay on top of your class attendance requirements. Enter your attended and total classes, set a target percentage, and instantly see where you stand.

    • Calculate your current attendance percentage
    • Find how many consecutive future classes you need to attend
    • Discover your safe absence allowance
    • Get color-coded attendance status feedback
    • Save your student profile locally in your browser
